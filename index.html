About Z&S Agro Enterprise

A registered agricultural company based in Nabweru, Uganda, specializing in farm produce trading, livestock management, and value addition services with a growing international presence.

Our Mission

To improve food systems through fair trade, sustainability, and innovative value addition while connecting farmers with global markets.

Our Vision

To become East Africa's leading agricultural enterprise, bridging the gap between local farmers and international markets through technology and innovation.

Our Values

Quality, integrity, sustainability, and farmer empowerment guide everything we do. We believe in building lasting partnerships and creating value for all stakeholders.

Our Services

Comprehensive agricultural solutions from farm to market

Agricultural Trading

Direct trade connections between farmers and bulk buyers for crops and livestock. We ensure fair pricing and quality standards.

Import & Export

International agro trade facilitation with quality handling, storage, and shipment to global markets.

Value Addition

Processing maize, beans, and poultry into ready-to-sell products for local and export markets.
