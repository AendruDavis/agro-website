About Us

A&S Agro Enterprise Ltd is a registered agricultural company based in Nabweru, Uganda. We specialize in the buying and selling of farm produce such as maize, beans, rice, and livestock including poultry, cattle, and goats.

With a growing regional presence, we engage in both local and international import/export of agro-products. Our vision is to improve food systems through fair trade, sustainability, and value addition.

Our Services

Agro Trading

We connect farmers with markets and bulk buyers through direct trade in crops and livestock.

Import & Export

We facilitate international agro trade, ensuring quality handling, storage, and shipment.

Value Addition

We process maize, beans, and poultry into ready-to-sell products for both local and export markets.

Our Products

Maize

Clean, sun-dried maize fit for flour milling and food processing.

Beans

Red and mixed beans, sorted, bagged and ready for market.

Rice

Unpolished and milled rice options available in bulk supply.

Poultry

Farm-raised chickens and fresh eggs delivered on order.

Cattle

Dairy and beef cattle, grass-fed and ready for sale or breeding.

Goats

Local and hybrid goats ideal for meat, trade, or breeding.

Value Addition

We are building processing capacity to turn raw farm produce into competitive, value-added products. These include:

  • Maize flour and animal feeds
  • Processed poultry (frozen meat)
  • Packed beans and rice for supermarkets
